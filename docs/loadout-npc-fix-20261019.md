# AutoPerk gold and prestige NPC correction — 2026-10-19

The saved server sidebar journal contained canonical lines such as `Gold: 77,078g` followed by an ogre, basketball or football emoji. The emoji was the unique scoreboard entry after the team prefix; the previous exact money match rejected the whole line. The gold sidebar parser now removes terminal Unicode decorative symbols only. Menu prices remain strict, duplicate gold lines remain invalid, and malformed numbers, extra amounts and arbitrary trailing text remain rejected. Tests also cover the reported `Gold: 5,699.54g` and Classic prefix/entry/suffix decimal formatting.

The previous NPC lookup matched any armor-stand text containing PRESTIGE and chose the nearest villager within ten blocks of the first matching label. It could associate instructional holograms or an adjacent shop with the prestige operation. The shared matcher now accepts exact shop labels (PRESTIGE, PRESTIGE & RENOWN, RESETS & RENOWN and their AND variants; UPGRADES/PERMANENT UPGRADES separately). A living villager must be within 1.5 blocks horizontally and beneath its label. Directly named villagers are supported. All valid labels are considered; horizontal alignment wins over proximity to the player. Queued native interactions recheck the intended operation and the currently resolved NPC.

Shared changes apply to Forge Nezur, native Ornithe and standalone AutoContractor/Grinder. Existing spawn, level, renown, affordability and purchase-confirmation checks remain in effect. No live multiplayer transaction is performed by the validation.
